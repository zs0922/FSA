pass. Native sat -seq is the independent temporal reference used for fixtures;
its expansion is internal to SAT and cannot be opt_merge-shared across frames.
Seven positive/negative fixtures compare native sequential SAT at time depth+1
(skipping the preceding depth outputs) against expanded combinational SAT.
The cases cover 3-stage DFF, active-high/low SDFF with nonzero reset values,

Five further fixtures assert rejection of other clocks, negative edges, unknown
cells, asynchronous registers, and initial-state-dependent cones.
The transformation accepts only an explicit combinational type whitelist and
requires every original register clock to be the sole top-level positive-edge
clock. It rejects undefined constants in the expanded cone.

This proof is not the original unmodified sat -tempinduct command, which is an
independent alternative and may remain an incomplete attempt. No claim is made about reciprocal or FSA scheduling.
